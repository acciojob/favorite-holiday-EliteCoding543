Manali


Manali is one of the most beautiful hill stations in India, located in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for its snow-covered mountains, adventure sports, scenic valleys, and peaceful weather. Tourists from all over the country visit Manali to enjoy nature, snowfall, and delicious local food.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley — famous for adventure sports like paragliding and skiing.
  2. Rohtang Pass — known for breathtaking snowy mountains.
  3. Hadimba Temple — an ancient temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali — popular for cafés, music, and peaceful vibes.
  5. Beas River — perfect for river rafting and sightseeing.

Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu — a traditional Himachali bread.
  • Thukpa — a hot noodle soup loved in cold weather.
  • Momos — steamed dumplings served with spicy chutney.
  • Trout Fish — a famous local delicacy in Manali.
  • Mittha — a sweet Himachali dessert.
